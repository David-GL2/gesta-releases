Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

GesTa is a Windows desktop application designed to manage tasks locally. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the application.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. GesTa does not request or store information such as names, email addresses, IP addresses or location data.

2. Local Storage

Tasks created in GesTa are stored locally in a tasks.json file within the application's folder.

This file remains on the user's computer and is not sent to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services

GesTa does not use third-party services intended to collect user data.

The application does not include behavioral analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

4. Internet Connection

GesTa is designed to work locally and does not require a permanent Internet connection to manage your tasks.

The application may require an Internet connection during the purchase or license verification process through Microsoft Store, depending on the platform's operation.

5. Security

Your tasks are stored locally on your own computer. GesTa does not copy this data to external servers.

The user is responsible for keeping their device secure and creating any backups they consider necessary.

6. Data Export

GesTa allows you to export your task information to formats such as CSV or PDF.

These exports are performed locally and remain under the user's control.

7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact the developer through the official channels associated with GesTa, including the Microsoft Store or the official GitHub repository.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the application or its features. Any modifications will be published on this page together with a new update date.
