Features

Everything you need to stay organized

The important features are here. No unnecessary tools or complicated menus.

TASKS

Complete task management

Create, edit and delete tasks easily. Mark them as completed when you're done. GesTa will highlight tasks that have passed their deadline.

ORGANIZATION

Built-in categories

Organize your tasks using predefined categories: uncategorized, work, studies, personal, diet, routine and other. Add deadlines when you need them — or don't.

SEARCH

Search and filter

Quickly find tasks by title, category, status (all, pending or completed) or date.

DATA

Local storage

Your tasks are stored locally in a JSON file inside the Documents folder from your user profile. You stay in control of your personal information.

EXPORT

Export your tasks

Export your information to CSV or PDF to keep a backup, analyze your data or even track your diet, workouts and other personal goals. Use GesTa as a small personal database.

DESIGN

Dark & minimalist

A dark, clean interface designed to be comfortable to use on your desktop.
